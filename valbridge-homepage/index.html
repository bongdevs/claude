How it works

A fair auction,
timed by the bidders.

No fixed end time and no last-second sniping. The clock starts with the first bid and every new bid gives everyone another 15 minutes. When the room goes quiet, the highest bid wins.

  1. 01

    Register & get approved

    Create an account and verify your e-mail. Our team approves bidders to keep the market serious.

  2. 02

    Bid or buy now

    Bid from the minimum price in fixed increments, or end the auction instantly at the Buy Now price.

  3. 03

    15 quiet minutes win

    Each bid resets the timer to 15:00. You get an instant e-mail and push if you are outbid.

  4. 04

    Pay & receive cold

    Pay securely by the deadline. Your lot ships in temperature-controlled packaging with batch traceability.

How does the 15-minute timer work?

The timer starts with the first bid on a lot. Every new valid bid resets it to 15:00. When it reaches zero without a new bid, the highest bidder wins and receives a payment link.

Are bids binding?

Yes. A bid is a binding offer and cannot be withdrawn. Please read the auction rules — including the payment deadline — before placing your first bid.

Who can bid?

Anyone can browse lots and prices. To bid or buy, register, verify your e-mail and wait for account approval. Restaurants and retailers can add a VAT ID for B2B invoicing.

How are the truffles shipped?

In temperature-controlled packaging by express carrier from Switzerland. Each delivery note carries batch and lot numbers, origin and storage instructions.

Which countries do you ship to?

We ship worldwide wherever food import rules allow. Your country's availability is shown at registration and on every lot page before you bid.

Be first when the next delivery lands.

One e-mail or push alert per delivery. No noise — unsubscribe any time.
